Delete Your Data or Account

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Auto-Mobile ("we," "our"), published by Bluelanternstudios, offers two separate options: Delete all data erases everything the app has stored on your device but keeps your anonymous account, and Delete Account removes your cloud-side account identifier and its subscription record. You can use either, both, or neither. Neither option requires creating a new account or logging in anywhere to request it.

Deleting your account removes your cloud-side account identifier immediately. Subscription-linked records held by our services are typically removed within 24 hours; in rare cases involving a third-party billing provider this can take longer, and we retry automatically for several days. If it still hasn't succeeded after that, it's flagged for our support team to complete manually — your account identifier is deleted immediately either way, so this only affects how quickly the linked subscription record is cleared.

Delete all data (on-device data, keeps your account)

This erases everything Auto-Mobile has saved on your device and cannot be undone. Your anonymous account and any active subscription are not affected.

  1. Open Auto-Mobile and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all data.
  3. Confirm at both prompts.
  4. Every vehicle, document, photo, receipt, maintenance and fuel record, parking spot, accident report, reminder and app setting stored on the device is deleted immediately, scheduled maintenance/parking reminders are cancelled, and your in-app annual usage allowances reset. Encrypted backup files you exported and saved elsewhere are not touched.

Delete Account — Option 1: from within the app

This is the fastest way if you still have Auto-Mobile installed.

  1. Open Auto-Mobile and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted (on a device with a passcode, fingerprint, or Face ID set up, you may also be asked to verify with it).
  4. Your account is deleted immediately. Note: this deletes your cloud account identifier and subscription record — data stored only on your device isn't affected by this step and can be removed separately by uninstalling the app or deleting individual records.

Delete Account — Option 2: request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Auto-Mobile installed, email us and we'll process the deletion on our end.

Send a deletion request to:
supportauto_mobile@bluelanternstudio.com

Your account is anonymous — we don't collect an email or name — so please include your Account ID so we can locate it. You can find this in the app under Settings > Account, just above the Delete Account button. We'll confirm once the deletion is complete, typically within 14 business days.
