Delete Your Account

Last updated: 18 August 2026

You can delete your Auto-Mobile account whether or not you still have the app installed. Deleting your account removes your cloud-side account identifier and any subscription-linked data held by our services — it does not require creating a new account or logging in anywhere to request it.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

This is the fastest way if you still have Auto-Mobile installed.

  1. Open Auto-Mobile and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted (you may be asked to verify with Face ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode).
  4. Your account is deleted immediately. Note: this deletes your cloud account identifier and subscription record — data stored only on your device isn't affected by this step and can be removed separately by uninstalling the app or deleting individual records.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Auto-Mobile installed, email us and we'll process the deletion on our end.

Send a deletion request to:
supportauto_mobile@bluelanternstudio.com

Please include, if you have it, the email or device identifier associated with your account so we can locate it. We'll confirm once the deletion is complete, typically within 14 business days.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted on account deletion?
Your anonymous cloud account identifierYes — removed immediately
Subscription/purchase records tied to your accountYes, from our systems — the App Store/Play Store retains its own purchase history per their policies
Vehicle records, photos, documents stored on your deviceNot automatically — this data lives only on your device. Uninstall the app or delete records individually to remove it
Any encrypted backup files you've exported and saved elsewhereNot automatically — you control where those files are saved and should delete them yourself if desired
